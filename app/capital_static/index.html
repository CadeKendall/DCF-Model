02 / THE METHODOLOGY

Every number
has a foundation.

Adapted from your cost-of-capital notebook, with the assumptions in view.

01

Measure the market relationship

Beta is stock–proxy covariance divided by proxy variance on shared daily returns. The proxy’s full-window return is compounded and annualized using 252 trading days.

02

Price the sources of capital

CAPM uses the latest FRED 10-year Treasury yield on or before your ending date. Debt cost is annual interest expense divided by average current and prior-year debt.

03

Bring the weights together

Fiscal-year-end equity is price × shares outstanding. Debt and equity weights combine their costs, with your selected tax rate applied to debt.

WACC = Wᴇ × Rᴇ + Wᴅ × Rᴅ × (1 − T)
